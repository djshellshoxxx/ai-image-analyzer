Raw validated C2PA JSON

Interpret negative results carefully. No metadata-based analyzer can prove that an image is human-made. Screenshots, social-media re-encodes, exports, transcoding, and deliberate metadata removal can destroy provenance. V2 distinguishes cryptographically validated C2PA evidence from ordinary mutable EXIF/XMP/IPTC metadata and from software fingerprints. A C2PA manifest by itself is not treated as evidence of AI generation.
